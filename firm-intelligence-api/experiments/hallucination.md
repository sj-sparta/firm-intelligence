## WHat is a hallucination?
- incidencts where AI produces outputs that sounds plausible but are factually wrong, irrelevant or entirely fabricated.
- intrinsic - generated output that has manipulated output that contradicts source material (who was first person on Mars -> Neil Armstrong)
- extrinsic - generated output has additional information not directly inferred from source material (policy says customer can get refund in 30 days -> AI says they can get refund AND a £50 voucher)
- extrinsic.... theres nothing in the source to contradict it
- example - fake disease on internet
- example - maths problem - plausable to believe as we would assume compauter correct at Maths


## Why do they happen?

- LLMs predict the next word not true ones - nothing in the process looks up or checks
- thin training data - rare, really specific info is not included
- large ambigious prompt
- Bad context in RAG - (...large context window, .... lost in the middle)
